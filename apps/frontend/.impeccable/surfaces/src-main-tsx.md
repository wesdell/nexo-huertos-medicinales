## Scope

App shell and all v1 screens of Nexo (login, registro, privacidad, inicio, comunidad nueva, huerto, actividad nueva, huerto virtual). Mode: Operate.

## Audience and task

Mixed garden communities using a phone outdoors in daylight. Core task: see today's lunar recommendation, register an activity, watch the virtual garden advance.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm, friendly consumer app where the only saturated color on screen is something you can press; it refuses the leaf-icon green-header gardening template and the stat-card dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Warm tinted ground (#FBF6EE) with white soft-cornered cards (radius 20px), one action color "verde chakra" #1F7A4D reserved for actionable elements and the active tab, warm ink #2A241D (chosen over the cool slate #1F2937 to stay in the warm world, as the quality-bar card does) and warm secondary #5B5348. Rounded humanist sans (Nunito) at comfortable sizes, plain Spanish labels. Lucide line icons, one stroke weight. Spring press feedback.

STORY: The user understands what the moon suggests today, believes the app helps their real garden, and registers one activity in under a minute.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile Inicio: brand mark with points top-right; greeting with community name; the focal "Hoy" card with a 96px equatorial moon drawing, the phase as headline, the recommendation, and the primary "Registrar actividad" button stating the 5-point bonus; the virtual garden card (plant drawings at their stage, progress and points in words, chevron to open). Quick-action row cut: it only repeated the bottom bar. Persistent floating bottom bar of four destinations, Registrar filled in the action color (third slot, kept next to the thumb rather than centered). Log out lives at the end of Inicio on mobile, away from accidental taps.

FORM: Warm consumer application surface (dealt challenger, chosen by user over the assigned "Rótulo de minga"); seed key d0ea0720.

SIGNATURE: After saving an activity, the plant's drawing springs to its new stage in the virtual garden while a toast states the points.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
